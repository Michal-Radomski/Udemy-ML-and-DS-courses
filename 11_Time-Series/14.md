Time series forecasting models help predict future values based on past data. They build on each other, starting from basic
autoregressive concepts up to advanced models that handle seasonal patterns and external factors.

---

### 1. AR (Autoregressive Model)

- **What it stands for:** Autoregressive
- **Core idea:** Forecasts a future value based on **its own past values**. It assumes that what happened in the recent past
  influences what happens next (like today's weather being related to yesterday's weather).
- **Key parameter:** $p$ (the number of past time steps used for the prediction, known as the lag order).

### 2. MA (Moving Average Model)

- **What it stands for:** Moving Average (not to be confused with a rolling average)
- **Core idea:** Forecasts future values based on **past forecast errors** (unexpected shocks or surprises in the data),
  rather than past raw values. It helps smooth out random noise in the series.
- **Key parameter:** $q$ (the size of the moving average window of past errors).

### 3. ARMA (Autoregressive Moving Average)

- **What it stands for:** Autoregressive Moving Average
- **Core idea:** Combines both **AR** and **MA** components into a single model. It looks at both past values and past errors
  to make predictions.
- **Limitation:** It only works for **stationary data**—meaning data whose mean, variance, and autocorrelation do not change
  over time.
- **Key parameters:** $(p, q)$ combining the AR lag order and MA error order.

### 4. ARIMA (Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average)

- **What it stands for:** Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average
- **Core idea:** Takes ARMA and adds an **"Integrated" (I)** step. This step involves _differencing_ the data (subtracting
  current values from previous ones) to remove trends and make non-stationary data stationary.
- **Key parameters:** $(p, d, q)$ where $d$ is the degree of differencing required to make the data stationary.

### 5. SARIMAX (Seasonal Autoregressive Integrated Moving-Average with Exogenous Regressors)

- **What it stands for:** Seasonal Autoregressive Integrated Moving-Average with Exogenous Regressors
- **Core idea:** The most comprehensive model in this family. It extends ARIMA by adding:
- **Seasonality (S):** Handles repeating patterns at fixed intervals (e.g., yearly holiday sales spikes or monthly cycles).
- **Exogenous Variables (X):** Incorporates external factors that affect the data (e.g., promotions, holidays, or
  temperature).

- **Key parameters:** $(p, d, q) \times (P, D, Q)_s$ for seasonal components, plus external variables.
