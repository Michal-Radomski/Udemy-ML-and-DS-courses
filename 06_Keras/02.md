MNIST is the Modified National Institute of Standards and Technology database, a foundational benchmark dataset in machine
learning containing 70,000 grayscale images of handwritten digits (0-9).
[botpenguin](https://botpenguin.com/glossary/mnist-dataset)

## Dataset Details

It includes 60,000 training images and 10,000 testing images, each standardized as 28×28 pixel grayscale images, making it
ideal for image classification tasks.
[csundergrad.science.uoit](https://csundergrad.science.uoit.ca/courses/deep-learning-primer/notebooks/mnist-dataset.html)

## Key Uses

- **Benchmark**: Standard "Hello World" for testing neural networks, CNNs, and computer vision algorithms.
- **Education**: Perfect for learning due to its simplicity and clear train/test split.
- **Research**: Enables fair algorithm comparisons with ~99% accuracy achievable by modern models.
