Underfitting and overfitting are core challenges in machine learning model training, describing poor generalization to new data. [geeksforgeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/machine-learning/underfitting-and-overfitting-in-machine-learning/)

## Underfitting
This happens when a model is too simple to capture underlying data patterns, leading to high error on both training and test sets (high bias, low variance). Causes include insufficient model complexity, weak features, excessive regularization, or inadequate training time. [ayadata](https://www.ayadata.ai/a-guide-to-overfitting-and-underfitting-in-machine-learning/)

## Overfitting
The model learns training data too well, including noise and outliers, excelling on training but failing on unseen test data (low bias, high variance). It arises from excessive model complexity, too few samples, noisy data, or lack of regularization. [ibm](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/overfitting-vs-underfitting)

## Bias-Variance Tradeoff
Balancing acts key: simple models underfit (high bias), complex ones overfit (high variance); aim for the "sweet spot" via validation curves. [developers.google](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/overfitting/overfitting)
